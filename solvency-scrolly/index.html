Who has solvency headroom?

Eleven insurers, one headline metric — how far above the regulatory floor does each sit on SCR coverage?

UK life insurers publish a solvency ratio every year. The one everyone watches first is SCR coverage — own funds as a percentage of the capital requirement.

Regulators set a hard floor at 100%. Below that, you're in breach. Above it, you're solvent — but how far above matters for headroom.

Here's the field — eleven insurers from the latest dashboard snapshot, sorted high to low. Every bar clears the floor. The question is by how much.

At the extremes: Rothesay tops the chart at 264%. Phoenix / Standard Life sits lowest at 120% — still solvent, but with the least cushion in this group.

The middle of the pack lands around 183% — the median SCR ratio. Most names cluster comfortably above the line, not hugging it.

Only two insurers sit below 150%: Phoenix and Scottish Widows (144%). Not a crisis — but tighter than peers.

A 144 percentage-point spread from top to bottom. On paper they all pass — but headroom is not evenly distributed.

Method

SCR coverage ratios from the SFCR dashboard snapshot (20 Jun 2026). Eleven UK life insurers with published ratios in the scanner set. Regulatory minimum 100% per Solvency II.
