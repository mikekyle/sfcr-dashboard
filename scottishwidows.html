Material Changes Noted

  • SWL obtained PRA approval to use the Matching Adjustment for bulk immediate annuities during 2016 (in addition to existing individual annuity approval)
  • SWL recalculated and restated its Transitional Measure on Technical Provisions as at 30 June 2016 following the fall in interest rates, and received approval in December 2016
  • Creation of the 'Longstanding Life, Protection and Investment' team
  • Establishment of a new Board committee, the Insurance People Committee
  • Creation of a Customer and Business Risk team (first line risk resource)
  • Insurance Model Governance Committee (IMGC) split into a Senior and Junior IMGC in February 2016
  • Merger of LP&I and GI Customer and Product Governance Committees into a single Insurance Customer and Product Committee
